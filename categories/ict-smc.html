What Are ICT/SMC/CRT?

ICT (Inner Circle Trader), SMC (Smart Money Concepts), and CRT (Candle Range Theory) are institutional trading methodologies. These channels analyze markets using order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps, and market structure shifts. These approaches aim to trade alongside institutional money rather than against it.

How to Choose the Right Channel

When evaluating ict/smc/crt strategy channels, consider:

  • Signal frequency — How often does the channel post trade signals?
  • Risk management — Does the channel provide stop-loss and take-profit levels?
  • Transparency — Does the channel share trade results and performance history?
  • Cost — Are the signals free or is there a VIP/premium tier?

Free vs. Premium Channels

Many channels listed here offer free access. Some also provide premium or VIP tiers with more frequent signals, mentorship, or exclusive analysis. Explore free options first before considering paid ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ICT in trading?
ICT (Inner Circle Trader) is a trading methodology focused on institutional market concepts and how large institutions move markets.
What is Smart Money Concepts (SMC)?
SMC analyzes market structure, order blocks, fair value gaps, and liquidity pools to identify institutional order placement.
What is CRT (Candle Range Theory)?
CRT uses the high-low range of specific candles to identify key decision points and potential trade entries.
